## Weekly topic-sourcing checklist (repeatable, not one-time)
1. **Google Trends** — check candidate topic name for rising interest (anniversaries/recent documentary mentions spike search).
2. **YouTube search suggest** — type the topic into YouTube's search bar, note autocompletes (real current query volume, free).
3. **Competitor upload velocity check** — weekly scan of recent uploads (LEMMiNO, Wendigoon, Nexpo, Dark5, MrBallen) for unusually fast early view counts vs. their normal average.
4. **vidIQ/TubeBuddy free tier** — keyword search-volume/competition score before committing a full production cycle.
5. **Anniversary/calendar awareness** — round-number anniversaries or new documentary/podcast mentions elsewhere often re-spike interest.

**Balance rule:** ~80% evergreen (this queue) / 20% trend-reactive slots filled based on live signals. Full trend-chasing would introduce exactly the volatility our calm/premium brand shouldn't have.

## Screening rule for future additions
Any topic involving a named real person's still-living relatives (modern missing-persons cases especially) gets a judgment check before scripting — flag explicitly in this table, don't just rely on memory.
