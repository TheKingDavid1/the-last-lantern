## Notes for Claude (weekly planning use)
- Keep at least 2 of every 3 upcoming topics at "Low" risk; "Medium" topics get an extra fact-check pass and extra care in tone (see persona.md consistency checklist).
- Avoid stacking two "Medium" risk weeks back-to-back early in the channel's life — spread them out once there's an established respectful tone to point to.
- Any topic involving a named real person's still-living relatives (e.g. modern missing-persons cases) should be run past a judgment check before scripting — none of the above 10 currently have that issue, but future additions should be screened for it explicitly.
