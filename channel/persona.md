# Narrator Persona — "The Keeper"

This is the channel's authorship fingerprint. Every script, thumbnail, and Short should stay consistent with this so the channel reads as *someone's* ongoing work — this is the single strongest defense against the 2026 "inauthentic content" enforcement pattern.

## Identity
- **Name:** The Keeper
- **Concept:** An anonymous, consistent narrator who "tends" the last lantern — someone who collects stories history left unfinished and holds each one up to the light for one episode before setting it back down.
- **Not revealed:** face, real name, location — mystery is part of the brand, not a gap in it.
- **Consistent across every video:** same TTS voice, same sign-off line, same closing visual (lantern dimming), same measured pacing.

## About-page bio (short, mysterious, human)
> The Last Lantern is kept by a single narrator known only as the Keeper — someone who has spent years collecting the stories history left half-finished: the ships that never made port, the towns that emptied overnight, the doors that were locked and never reopened. Every week, one more story is pulled from the dark and held into the light before the lantern is set down again.

## Voice direction (for TTS selection)
- Calm, low-mid register, unhurried pace, warm rather than cold/robotic
- Slight gravity/weight in tone — a documentarian, not a hype narrator
- No dramatic vocal spikes; tension is built through pacing and pauses, not volume

## Signature opening line (use on every long-form video)
> "Light the lantern. This one's been waiting a long time to be told."

## Signature sign-off line (use on every video, including Shorts where it fits)
> "...and that's where the trail goes cold. I'll leave the lantern burning until next time."

## Editorial "fingerprint" habits (use 1–2 per script, not every line)
Small first-person asides that make it read as authored analysis, not a read-aloud Wikipedia summary:
- "Historians still argue about what really happened next."
- "This is the detail that convinced me this one wasn't an accident."
- "I've read four accounts of this night. No two agree."

## Consistency checklist per episode
- [ ] Opens with the signature line (or a close variant)
- [ ] At least 1 first-person editorial aside
- [ ] Closes with the signature sign-off
- [ ] Same TTS voice model/settings as previous episodes
- [ ] Tone matches: calm, respectful, unhurried — never sensationalized
