## Full About page description

The Last Lantern is a home for stories the world set down and forgot to pick back up — the ships that never made port, the towns that emptied overnight, the places history left standing but empty.

Every story here is true, carefully researched, and told slowly, on purpose: low light, quiet pacing, calm narration. Some episodes are built to keep your attention gently through a strange piece of history. Others are longer, slower "sleep stories," meant to run in the background as you drift off.

New episodes weekly, narrated by the Keeper — the voice who tends this channel's lantern and decides which forgotten story gets pulled into the light next.

**What you'll find here:**
- Unsolved disappearances — ships, expeditions, people who vanished without explanation
- Forgotten historical events with an eerie edge
- Abandoned places with a real, documented backstory
- Slow "sleep story" retellings, built for falling asleep to

New story every week. Settle in, dim the lights, and let the lantern burn low.
